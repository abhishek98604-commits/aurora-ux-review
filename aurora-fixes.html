Aurora UX review

What I did
  • Signed up. It was quick and easy.
  • Ran five real searches: a café to work from, a salon, a dentist checkup, a maths tutor and a 3D printing job.
  • Read the Play Store and App Store reviews and checked them against my own issues.
  • Ran the same searches in Claude to see what a normal AI search gives you.
Overall impression
  • The UI is clean, and the follow-up questions do narrow the search well.
  • The dentist search was a real win. It found a slot and the clinic called me back. Nothing else I tried could do that.
  • It's slow. The café search ran 38 calls and widened to 15 km before showing anything. One search kept going with no result.
  • I was not sure why I would use it over Google. For the café and salon, Google or an AI chat gave most of the answer in seconds.
What reviewers say
  • Still few reviews: 4.5 from 20 on Google Play, 4.3 from 6 on the App Store.
  • People like the time saved and getting current prices rather than outdated listings.
  • The complaints are about trust in status: one user says it stopped making calls and gave excuses, another that a reservation stayed "in progress" and never went through. These match issues 4 and 6 below.
  • One review asked for voice. That now exists, but in my use it misheard short replies (issue 3).
